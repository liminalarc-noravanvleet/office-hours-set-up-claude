# Why Use The Terminal

- Clients' engineering teams work in the Terminal every day.
- AI tools running in the Terminal can work directly with your files, automating multi-step tasks like reorganizing documents or updating deliverables in one go.
- Compared to AI in an app or web browser, Terminal-based AI skips the copy-and-paste, keeps context across an entire project, and handles longer tasks with less back-and-forth.

# What Is the Terminal?

- The Terminal is a built-in Mac app that lets you control your computer by typing text commands instead of clicking buttons and menus.
- Many developer and AI tools, like Homebrew and Git, are installed and run through the Terminal.

# How to Install Homebrew on a Mac

- Open **Terminal** (Applications > Utilities > Terminal).
- Paste and run: `/bin/bash -c "$(curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Homebrew/install/HEAD/install.sh)"`, then enter your Mac password when prompted.
- Run the commands shown under **Next steps**, then confirm it worked with `brew --version`.
